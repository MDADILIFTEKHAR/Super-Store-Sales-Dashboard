# 📊 E-Commerce Sales Dashboard | Power BI

An interactive Business Intelligence dashboard built using **Microsoft Power BI** to analyze and visualize e-commerce sales performance. This project transforms raw sales data into meaningful insights through dynamic visualizations, helping businesses monitor KPIs, identify trends, and make data-driven decisions.

---

## 📌 Project Overview

The E-Commerce Sales Dashboard provides a complete overview of business performance by analyzing sales, profit, customer behavior, shipping methods, payment modes, product categories, and regional performance.

The dashboard enables decision-makers to monitor business growth, identify high-performing products, understand customer preferences, and optimize sales strategies through interactive reports.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

- Analyze overall sales performance
- Track profit and revenue trends
- Compare sales across different regions and states
- Identify top-performing product categories
- Understand customer purchasing behavior
- Analyze shipping and payment preferences
- Enable interactive business reporting using filters and slicers
